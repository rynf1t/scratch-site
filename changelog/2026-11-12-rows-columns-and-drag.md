# Insert and delete rows and columns (2026-11-12)

**Insert rows, insert columns, or delete either.** Open **Rows and Columns** in the sheet menu or look up the command in the palette. Formulas and linked sources follow the cells that move. Undo restores the data and its links. [Rows and columns guide](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#insert-or-delete-rows-and-columns).

**Drag a selection from the export icon.** It sits beside the sheet name. Drag it to share the selection, or click it to save a CSV. [Export guide](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#drag-out-a-table-or-export-csv).
