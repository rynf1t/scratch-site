# More room for the grid and saved Obsidian sheets (2026-11-12)

**Click Expand to see more columns.** Notes can stay open while the window fills the usable display. Click **Compact** to return to your previous size. [Grid and shortcuts](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md).

**Send to Obsidian saves the sheet too.** A full send updates the dated copy in `Scratch/Sheets` and adds the table and notes to today’s daily note. The selection command still sends just the selected range and notes. [Notes and Obsidian](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/notes-and-obsidian.md).
