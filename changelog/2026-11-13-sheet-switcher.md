# Find a sheet or cycle straight to it (2026-11-13)

**Press ⌘K to find a sheet.** The sheets you recently visited appear first, with the last selected cell beside each name. Type to narrow the list or press Return to open the first result.

**Press ⌃Tab to move to the next sheet or ⌃⇧Tab to go back.** Scratch wraps around the workbook and skips archived sheets. Each sheet keeps its selection and scroll position.

[Keyboard shortcuts](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/shortcuts.md#switch-sheets)
