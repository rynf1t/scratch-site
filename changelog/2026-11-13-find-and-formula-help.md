# Find your way back and see formula arguments (2026-11-13)

**Press ⌘F then Return to get back after opening a Find result.** Scratch restores your grid selection and scroll position, or your place in Notes. The same action is available as **Back to Find Origin** in the command palette.

**Formula suggestions show the argument names and what the function does.** Type `=su` in a cell to see `SUM(values, …)`. Press Tab or click the suggestion to insert the function and keep typing.
