See where your tables are

Start a named table from a selection or a filled cell. Scratch previews the range and tells you which row becomes the header. After you name it, the tinted header and outline show where the table sits in the sheet.

Docked Notes opens and closes from the divider. Reopen it to continue reading or editing where you left off. Nearby jumps scroll into view, and distant jumps use a short fade. You can choose the pace under Animation Style or use macOS Reduce Motion.

To bring edits back from Obsidian, edit the note in Scratch/Editable Tables and run Sync Editable Table with Obsidian in Scratch. Use Scratch/Sheets for dated snapshots.
