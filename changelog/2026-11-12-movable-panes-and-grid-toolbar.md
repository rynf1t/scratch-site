# Movable panes and a visible grid toolbar (2026-11-12)

**Move the grid and Notes.** Drag either grip to a highlighted edge to swap sides or dock. Drop outside to separate the windows. Scratch remembers the layout and window positions. Click a grip for the same commands. [Move or detach panes](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#move-or-detach-panes).

**Find the grid tools above the formula bar.** Click **Freeze**, **Sort**, **Filter** or **Export**. The Export button offers the selection or sheet as a CSV and still works as a drag handle. [Grid toolbar](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#grid-toolbar).
