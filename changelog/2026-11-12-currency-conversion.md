# Convert currency from the palette (2026-11-12)

**Type `100 AUD to USD` in the command palette.** Fetch the ECB rates, then copy the converted number. Scratch shows the exchange rate and its date, and keeps the last fetched rates for offline use. [Convert currency](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#convert-currency).
