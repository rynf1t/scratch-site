# Open the source of a Scratch export (2026-11-12)

**Click Open in Scratch to return to an export’s source.** A sheet export opens the sheet. A selection sent to the daily note opens the original range. [Open an export in Scratch](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/notes-and-obsidian.md#open-an-export-in-scratch).
