# Copy a range as Markdown, a total or an image (2026-11-13)

**Copy Selection As gives you three ways to share a range.** Paste a Markdown table into Obsidian, send the total in a sentence or paste a clean PNG of the cells. Open it from Export or the command palette.

**Export groups its actions by selection, table, sheet and workbook.** The Selection heading shows the current range. When the active cell is in a named table, the Table heading shows its name.
