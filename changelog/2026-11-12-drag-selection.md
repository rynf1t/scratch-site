# Drag across more of the grid (2026-11-12)

**Hold a drag at the grid edge to keep scrolling.** The range follows the pointer as rows or columns come into view. Move back inside the grid to pause scrolling, or release to finish.

This works for ordinary and Shift selection, the fill handle and formula references. [Grid and shortcuts](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#move-and-select).
