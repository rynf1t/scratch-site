# Record commands and customise shortcuts (2026-11-12)

**Record a sequence of grid commands.** Save up to 12 fill, format and clear steps, then preview them on another range and apply them as one undoable edit. [Record and Replay](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/replay.md).

**Choose your Notes and palette shortcuts.** Open **⋯ → Keyboard Shortcuts** to change the bindings for the command palette, Notes, pane focus and sending to Obsidian. [Keyboard Shortcuts](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/shortcuts.md).
