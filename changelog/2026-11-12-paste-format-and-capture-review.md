# Preview paste, browse formats and review captures (2026-11-12)

**Adjust a paste before applying it.** Open Preview Paste to see the destination, dimensions and occupied cells. Move the preview and choose Values, Formulas, Transpose or Split. [Paste guide](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/capture.md#choose-how-to-paste).

**See formats while browsing.** Highlight an item in the Format menu to preview it on the selected cells. Choose it to apply, or press Escape to restore the original appearance. [Format guide](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/grid.md#format-a-range).

**Review and append screenshot amounts.** Check each amount against its source crop and compare the sum with the printed total. Mark rows reviewed, then append them to mapped columns in another sheet. Scratch remembers the mapping. [Capture guide](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/capture.md#read-a-screenshot).
