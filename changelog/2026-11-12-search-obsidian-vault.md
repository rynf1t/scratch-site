# Search your Obsidian vault from Scratch (2026-11-12)

**Choose Search Obsidian Vault in the command palette.** Results show a matching snippet and folder. Press Return to open a note in Obsidian or ⌥Return to insert its link into Scratch Notes. [Search your Obsidian vault](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/notes-and-obsidian.md#search-your-obsidian-vault).
