# Resize the Notes pane (2026-11-12)

**Drag the divider to give the grid or Notes more room.** Double-click it to reset the width. You can also use **Widen Notes**, **Narrow Notes**, or **Reset Notes Width** from the command palette.

Scratch remembers the width when you close Notes or quit the app. [Resize Notes](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/notes-and-obsidian.md#resize-notes).
