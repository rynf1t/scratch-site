# Selection History (2026-11-12)

**Recover an earlier range of cells.** Select the range and choose **Show Selection History** to compare its old and current entries, formulas and formatting. Restore the range in one undoable edit. Scratch saves the history with the workbook. [Selection History](https://scratch.itsryan.me/docs/history.md).
