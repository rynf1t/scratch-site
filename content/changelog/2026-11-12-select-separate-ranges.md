---
title: Select separate cells and ranges
description: Format, clear or total cells across several ranges in one selection.
---

**Hold ⌘ to add a cell or drag out another range.** You can also enter an address with **Add Range to Selection** in the command palette.

Format or clear the selected cells in one edit, or use **Copy Result** to copy their total. Press Escape to keep the latest range. [Select separate ranges](/docs/grid#select-separate-ranges).
