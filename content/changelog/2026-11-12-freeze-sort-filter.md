---
title: Freeze panes, sort and filter
description: Keep labels visible, sort complete rows and narrow a sheet by what you see in a column.
---

**Keep labels in view as you scroll.** Freeze the rows above and columns to the left of your selection. Drag either divider’s blue grip to move the boundary. Scratch remembers each sheet’s setting. [Freeze panes](/docs/grid#freeze-rows-and-columns).

**Sort rows together.** Pick a column and review the row bounds before applying. Formulas, linked notes and annotations follow their source cells. Undo restores the original order. [Sort rows](/docs/grid#sort-rows).

**Filter by what you see.** Enter part of a value and move through the matching rows with the keyboard. Click the match count in the footer to clear the filter. CSV exports and Obsidian sync keep the hidden rows. [Filter rows](/docs/grid#filter-rows).
