---
title: Record commands and customise shortcuts
description: Repeat grid commands and choose shortcuts that fit your Mac.
---

**Record a sequence of grid commands.** Save up to 12 fill, format and clear steps, then preview them on another range and apply them as one undoable edit. [Record and Replay](/docs/replay).

**Choose your Notes and palette shortcuts.** Open **⋯ → Keyboard Shortcuts** to change the bindings for the command palette, Notes, pane focus and sending to Obsidian. [Keyboard Shortcuts](/docs/shortcuts).
