---
title: Linked annotations, sheet zoom and clearer selection
description: Edit a shared annotation from Notes or the grid, zoom sheets and preview range drags.
---

**See and edit annotations from either pane.** Option-drag selected note text to the grid to create a linked annotation. Click the text in Notes or open the annotation from the grid to edit it. The Notes footer shows how many annotations are on the sheet. [Notes and annotations](/docs/notes-and-obsidian#annotate-a-cell-or-range).

**Read formatted Notes and return from linked cells.** Headings, emphasis and escaped punctuation render in place. Hover over a live value to see its source. After following a link, press ⌃⌘← to return to the note selection and scroll position. [Notes guide](/docs/notes-and-obsidian).

**Resize and zoom each sheet.** Column dividers show a grip on hover, and the Notes divider is easier to grab. Sheet zoom scales text and grid controls and is remembered per sheet. [Grid guide](/docs/grid#format-a-range).

**See what a range drag will do.** Selection and fill show dimensions. Drag a selected range’s border to move it, or hold Option to copy it. Scratch previews the destination and any shifted formulas. [Selection guide](/docs/grid#move-and-select).
