---
title: Insert and delete rows and columns
description: Make room in a sheet and drag a selection from the toolbar.
---

**Insert rows, insert columns, or delete either.** Open **Rows and Columns** in the sheet menu or look up the command in the palette. Formulas and linked sources follow the cells that move. Undo restores the data and its links. [Rows and columns guide](/docs/grid#insert-or-delete-rows-and-columns).

**Drag a selection from the export icon.** It sits beside the sheet name. Drag it to share the selection, or click it to save a CSV. [Export guide](/docs/grid#drag-out-a-table-or-export-csv).
