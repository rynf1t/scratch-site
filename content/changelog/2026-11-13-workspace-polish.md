---
title: A cleaner workspace
description: A lighter grid, a roomier palette, and controls that line up across Scratch.
---

**The grid, formula bar and Notes use aligned controls and consistent insets.** The palette fits its results and highlights matching text. Choose **Natural**, **Snappy** or **Off** under **Animation Style**. Use **Follow System Accent** to match macOS.

**Preview a paste without shifting the grid.** The bar shows the paste modes and flags cells you’ll replace. Capture review has previous and next controls, a **Mark Correct** button and a progress bar beside the source.

**Trace a formula to the cell behind an error.** The microscope shows the chain and a jump to the source. The expanded formula bar pairs each reference colour with the cells it covers. Selection History highlights changes to entries and formats.

**Notes keeps the Markdown and shows a quieter view while you edit.** Lists show bullets and checkboxes. Live tables use a text header when one is present, and numeric values line up at the right. Hover over Send to see what will go to Obsidian. A confirmed send leaves a receipt and a link.

**Undo a sheet deletion from the footer.** Click **Undo** or press ⌘Z after deleting a sheet. Use **Restore Deleted Sheet** to bring it back later.
