---
title: Resize the Notes pane
description: Adjust the divider between the grid and Notes. Scratch remembers your width.
---

**Drag the divider to give the grid or Notes more room.** Double-click it to reset the width. You can also use **Widen Notes**, **Narrow Notes**, or **Reset Notes Width** from the command palette.

Scratch remembers the width when you close Notes or quit the app. [Resize Notes](/docs/notes-and-obsidian#resize-notes).
