---
title: Obsidian destinations, result sources and CSV
description: See where a send landed, trace a pinned result, and drag sheets and selections out of Scratch.
---

**See where your Obsidian send landed.** The Notes pane shows the vault and delivery mode. After a confirmed send, use Open to go to the note. Editable sync shows conflicting values side by side so you can pick what to keep. [Notes and Obsidian guide](/docs/notes-and-obsidian).

**Trace a pinned result.** Expand the floating card to see its formula and inputs. Jump straight to a cell, range or screenshot source from the card. [Formula guide](/docs/formula-microscope#see-where-a-pinned-result-came-from).

**Drag out a sheet or save a CSV.** Drag the sheet name beyond the panel to open a larger live view. Control-drag a selection or sheet name to share a table or drop a CSV in Finder. You can also export CSV from the command palette. [Grid guide](/docs/grid#drag-out-a-table-or-export-csv).
