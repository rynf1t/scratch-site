---
title: Named ranges, sheet copies and file import
description: Save a range by name, duplicate a sheet, and open CSV and TSV files.
---

**Jump back to a named range.** Name a selection from the sheet menu or command palette. Use **Go to Named Range** to find it again, even after rows or columns move. [Named ranges guide](/docs/grid#save-a-named-range).

**Duplicate a sheet or import a file.** Make a copy with its formulas, formatting and notes. Use **Import CSV or TSV** to open a file in its own sheet. [Sheet guide](/docs/grid#duplicate-a-sheet-or-import-a-file).
