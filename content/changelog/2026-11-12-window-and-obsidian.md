---
title: More room for the grid and saved Obsidian sheets
description: Expand from the toolbar and save a sheet copy when you send to Obsidian.
---

**Click Expand to see more columns.** Notes can stay open while the window fills the usable display. Click **Compact** to return to your previous size. [Grid and shortcuts](/docs/grid).

**Send to Obsidian saves the sheet too.** A full send updates the dated copy in `Scratch/Sheets` and adds the table and notes to today’s daily note. The selection command still sends just the selected range and notes. [Notes and Obsidian](/docs/notes-and-obsidian).
