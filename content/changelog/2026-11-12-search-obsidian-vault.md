---
title: Search your Obsidian vault from Scratch
description: Find notes by title or text, open a result, or insert its link into Scratch Notes.
---

**Choose Search Obsidian Vault in the command palette.** Results show a matching snippet and folder. Press Return to open a note in Obsidian or ⌥Return to insert its link into Scratch Notes. [Search your Obsidian vault](/docs/notes-and-obsidian#search-your-obsidian-vault).
