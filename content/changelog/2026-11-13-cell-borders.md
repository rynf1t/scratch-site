---
title: Borders and table styling
description: Preview borders, headers, alternating rows and a totals rule in the grid.
---

**Preview cell borders from Format.** Select a range and choose outside edges, inside lines, all edges or a bottom rule. Use Clear borders to remove them. Apply the change in one step and press ⌘Z to undo it.

**Format as Table** adds an optional header, alternating rows and a rule above the last row. The preview shows the range before you apply it. Formulas and existing table names stay with the sheet.
