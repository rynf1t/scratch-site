**Inspect the steps behind a formula.** Click ƒx to see intermediate results, follow references or jump to the cell contributing to an error. [Formula Microscope](/docs/formula-microscope).

**Make room for a larger sheet.** Drag the grip at the lower right, or use **Expand Window** and **Restore Compact Window** from the command palette.

**Drag ranges into a formula.** Adjacent cells stay available while you type a long formula. Try typing `=SUM(` and dragging over the cells to add. [Grid and shortcuts](/docs/grid).
