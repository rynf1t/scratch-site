---
title: Nudge a number or date from the keyboard
description: Adjust the selected cell and see formulas update as you hold the key.
---

**Press ⌃⌥↑ or ⌃⌥↓ to nudge the active cell.** Numbers move by one, dates by a day and percentages by a percentage point. Add Shift for a larger step. Hold the key to keep adjusting, then press ⌘Z to undo the full adjustment.

**Empty sheets show one first-run hint at a time.** Start typing, open the command palette or use Escape to hide Scratch. Each hint retires after you use it.
