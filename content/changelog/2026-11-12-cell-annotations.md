title: Annotate cells and ranges
description: Leave a note on a specific part of the grid and jump back to it later.
---

**Choose Annotate Selection to leave a note on a cell or range.** Click the marker to reopen it, or use **Find Annotations** to jump to it from the command palette. [Annotate a cell or range](/docs/notes-and-obsidian#annotate-a-cell-or-range).
