---
title: Notes on every edge
description: Dock Notes above or below the grid, narrow a sort to specific rows and open editable tables cleanly in Obsidian.
---

**Drag Notes to the top, bottom, left or right edge, or drag it out into its own window.** Scratch remembers a separate size for each edge and keeps your grid selection, Notes caret and scroll position as you move it. Drag the dotted divider to resize. Double-click it to reset.

Sort now shows **Rows to move**. To sort amounts in rows 2 through 4 and keep a total on row 5, enter `2:4`. When a sort would break up a formula range, the preview names the formula cell and its range.

**Annotate Selection** puts the caret in the editor when it opens. Editable Obsidian notes now give the Markdown table its own block. Sync an older editable note again to update it in place.
