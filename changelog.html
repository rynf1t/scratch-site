  2. 13 NOV 2026

    See where your tables are

    Start a named table from a selection or a filled cell. Scratch previews the range and tells you which row becomes the header. After you name it, the tinted header and outline show where the table sits in the sheet.

  3. 13 NOV 2026

    Find a sheet or cycle straight to it

    Press ⌘K to find a sheet. The sheets you recently visited appear first, with the last selected cell beside each name. Type to narrow the list or press Return to open the first result.

  4. 13 NOV 2026

    Notes on every edge

    Drag Notes to the top, bottom, left or right edge, or drag it out into its own window. Scratch remembers a separate size for each edge and keeps your grid selection, Notes caret and scroll position as you move it. Drag the dotted divider to resize. Double-click it to reset.

  6. 13 NOV 2026

    Find your way back and see formula arguments

    Press ⌘F then Return to get back after opening a Find result. Scratch restores your grid selection and scroll position, or your place in Notes. The same action is available as Back to Find Origin in the command palette.

  9. 13 NOV 2026

    Nudge a number or date from the keyboard

    Press ⌃⌥↑ or ⌃⌥↓ to nudge the active cell. Numbers move by one, dates by a day and percentages by a percentage point. Add Shift for a larger step. Hold the key to keep adjusting, then press ⌘Z to undo the full adjustment.
