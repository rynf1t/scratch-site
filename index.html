Bring numbers in from another app

Copy amounts or a screenshot and press ⌥⇧ Space. Scratch opens a new sheet. For screenshots, select a row to check the recognised amount against the source image.

Questions

Can I download Scratch?
Scratch is a local preview. A public app download is still pending.
Where do my sheets live?
Scratch saves the workbook and source images on your Mac. Screenshot recognition also runs on your Mac.
Can I read these guides as Markdown?
Yes. Add .md to a guide URL, or start at llms.txt.
